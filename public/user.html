Help & Support

Frequently asked questions
How can I book an event?

Open All Events, select an event and click Book Now.

How do I know whether my booking is approved?

The booking status appears in My Bookings and Booking History.

Where can I find my QR Entry Pass?

Approved bookings show the QR in My Bookings under Show entry QR pass.

How do I scan my QR code?

Open the QR with your camera or QR scanner and allow the verification page to load.

What if my QR has already been scanned?

The verification page displays Already Scanned - Entry Completed and entry is not recorded twice.

How can I cancel my booking?

Use the cancellation option shown for eligible bookings, if available.
